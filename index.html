How to install (about 1 minute)

  1. Tap Download. If Chrome says the file "can harm your device", tap Download anyway. That message appears for every app that is not from the Play Store.
  2. Open the file when the download finishes — tap Open at the bottom of Chrome, or open it from your notifications or the Files app → Downloads.
  3. Allow the install if your phone asks. Tap Settings, switch on Allow from this source, then go back and tap Install.
  4. If Play Protect shows a warning, tap More details and then Install anyway. Gyan is a private app shared by invitation, so Google has not reviewed it.
  5. Open Gyan, create your account with your email, and enter the invite code from above.

Already have Gyan?

The app updates itself. When it tells you a new version is ready, tap Download — installing it over the old one keeps your account and chats. You can also download the newest version from this page any time.
